FAQ

RootRecord apps and rootrecord.info account

Quick answers for RootRecord desktop programs (Windows) and your rootrecord.info account used with online features.

Install and updates (Windows)

How do I install?
Each program lists its download on its own page. Start from Products, then open RootRecord Business Manager or Root Record Weather Manager for the latest installer links.

Web account

How do I create an account?
Open Account on this site to sign up or sign in. If you use the rootrecord.info web app, you can also use Sign up and manage your profile at Account.

What plans exist?
See Pricing for Free, Monthly, and Lifetime in plain language. The Billing page and Terms have the full legal detail. Your desktop apps may use the same account and plan where the product is set up that way.

Trials and renewals?
Trial and renewal rules for eligible plans are on Billing and in Terms. Recurring plans bill through our payment provider until you cancel as described there.

RootRecord Business Manager (desktop)

Where is my data stored?
Your operational database file is stored locally on your computer (the app shows the path). Back it up like any important file; the app can run scheduled local backups.

Do I need an account?
No for core time, money, clients, inventory, and reports. A RootRecord account is used for subscription, sync, and optional secure online backup copy when you enable them.

Why do “hours worked” totals differ from a raw sum of rows?
Overlapping clock intervals are merged for unique clock coverage so you are not double-counted when two blocks share the same minutes.

Can I use the app offline?
Yes for core workflows. Sign-in, sync, and secure backup copy need connectivity when you use them.

What if my trial ends or payment is past due?
The app may switch to read-only until billing is current; use Billing on the web or in-app links to fix payment, then refresh status.
