Root Record Weather Manager

One calm desktop window for weather, alerts, earthquakes, and hazard context

Built for people who want clarity without juggling a dozen browser tabs. Your locations, history, and database stay on your machine.

Install (Windows)

Installers are published on GitHub Releases with each version. Signed builds use Authenticode where offered, which helps on strict PCs and with Smart App Control.

Download latest release

Release notes and version history: rootrecord-weather-manager-download. The running build is shown under About in the app.

What it covers

  • Weather dashboard, alerts, radar and satellite layers, and forecast periods for saved locations.
  • Earthquakes and tsunami bulletins, cyclone and wildfire feeds from public sources.
  • Critical popups (where included with your plan) for high-severity weather and USGS events near you.
  • RootRecord account sign-in for subscription and online features, aligned with other RootRecord apps.
