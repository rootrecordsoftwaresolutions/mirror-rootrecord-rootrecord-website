RootRecord Business Manager

Local-first operations on your Windows PC

Time, money, clients, stock, scheduling, work log, and reports in one desktop workspace. Your primary database file stays on your machine; sync and secure online backup are optional when you enable them.

Install (Windows)

GitHub Releases hosts the latest Windows .exe installer for each version.

Download installer (GitHub)

In the app, About & Help shows your build and documentation.

What you get

  • Time tracking and work log with sensible totals (overlapping clock intervals are merged so minutes are not double-counted).
  • Money and clients, invoices, categories, projects, and tags.
  • Inventory and supplies, schedule, and reports / exports you can stand behind.
  • Offline-first core workflows; sign-in for subscription, sync, and optional cloud backup when you use those features.
