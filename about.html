About

Capable tools, without giving up control.

RootRecord builds Windows desktop software for people who want real operational software and want their data to stay theirs.

Product-specific features, install paths, and screenshots live on each program's page under Products.

How we think about data

Where a product uses a local database, that file stays on your computer unless you move it or use optional features that explicitly copy or sync data you have configured. Core workflows are designed to work offline; online sign-in is for subscription, sync, backup, or other services when you choose them.

Web account

rootrecord.info hosts sign-in, account settings, and billing flows that tie into our apps where configured. Use Account from your browser to check status and manage billing.
